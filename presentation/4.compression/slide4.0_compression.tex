\begin{frame}{Compression}
    Operations like addition and multiplication forcefully 
    increase the rang of the generators.

    \[
        G =
        \begin{pmatrix}
            4 & \cdots \\
            5 & \cdots \\
            6 & \cdots \\
            7 & \cdots
        \end{pmatrix}
        \qquad
        H^{T} =
        \begin{pmatrix}
            1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
            \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots
        \end{pmatrix}
    \]
\end{frame}
