\begin{frame}{Compression}
  Operations like addition and multiplication artificially
  increase the rank of the generator matrices.

  \[
    T =
    \begin{pmatrix}
      4 & 0 & 4 & 0 \\
      5 & 1 & 5 & 1 \\
      6 & 0 & 6 & 0 \\
      7 & 0 & 7 & 0
    \end{pmatrix}
    U =
    \begin{pmatrix}
      0 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\
      3 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\
      2 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\
      1 & 0 & 1 & 0
    \end{pmatrix}
  \]
  represents:
  \[
    \begin{pmatrix}
      8 & 6 & 4 & 2 \\
      10 & 8 & 6 & 4 \\
      12 & 10 & 8 & 6 \\
      14 & 12 & 10 & 8
    \end{pmatrix}
  \]
\end{frame}
